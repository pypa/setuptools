``setuptools.archive_util.unpack_tarfile`` now vets every tar member through
``tarfile.data_filter`` (PEP 706) before extracting it, inheriting the
stdlib's data-extraction hardening (sanitized permission bits, dropped
ownership, rejected absolute/escaping link targets and special files)
instead of extracting through private ``tarfile`` APIs that bypassed the
filters. A member rejected by the filter now aborts the extraction with an
``UnsafeMember`` error naming the member, instead of being silently
skipped.
