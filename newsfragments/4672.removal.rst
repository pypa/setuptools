Dash-separated and uppercase keys in ``setup.cfg`` are no longer accepted and now
raise ``InvalidConfigError``. Previously they were normalized to their underscored
lowercase equivalents with a deprecation warning, first issued in 2021. Replace
affected keys with the underscored lowercase spelling, e.g. ``author_email``
instead of ``author-email`` and ``name`` instead of ``Name``. Keys in
``options.extras_require``, ``options.data_files``, ``options.entry_points``,
``options.package_data`` and ``options.exclude_package_data`` are unaffected, as
are sections that do not belong to setuptools. See
https://github.com/pypa/setuptools/discussions/5011 for details.
