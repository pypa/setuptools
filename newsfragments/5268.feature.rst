The compilers no longer depend on ``distutils.util``, ``distutils.version``, ``distutils.compat``, or ``distutils._macos_compat``. The platform-identification helpers (``get_platform``/``get_host_platform``/``is_mingw``) now live in ``distutils.compilers.platform.detect`` and the macOS deployment-target logic and ``compiler_fixup`` in ``distutils.compilers.platform.macos``; ``CygwinCCompiler.gcc_version`` returns a ``packaging.version.Version``. ``distutils.util`` re-exports the platform/macOS helpers from their new homes for backward compatibility rather than keeping duplicate copies. (sysconfig lookups still route through distutils pending its own decoupling.)
