The compilers no longer depend on ``distutils.util``, ``distutils.version``, ``distutils.compat``, or ``distutils._macos_compat``. The platform-identification helpers (``get_platform``/``get_host_platform``/``is_mingw``) are vendored into ``distutils.compilers.platform.detect``; the macOS deployment-target logic and ``compiler_fixup`` into ``distutils.compilers.platform.macos``; ``consolidate_linker_args`` into ``distutils.compilers._util``; and ``CygwinCCompiler.gcc_version`` now returns a ``packaging.version.Version``. (sysconfig lookups still route through distutils pending its own decoupling.)
